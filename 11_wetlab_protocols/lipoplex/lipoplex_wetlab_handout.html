Lipoplex surface targeting, wet-lab handout

Which receptor to put on the particle, which antibody to order, and what to check first.
Prepared 12 August 2026 · computational assessment, no wet-lab data · supersedes nothing in antibody_assessment.md, extends it.
Bottom line Use TfR + P2X3 as the targeting pair. Drop TfR + TRPV1, TRPV1 has almost no extracellular surface for an antibody to bind on an intact cell. No P2X3 antibody currently on the market is validated for live-cell surface binding. The first experiment is therefore a surface-binding test, not a formulation study.

1 · Why P2X3 and not TRPV1

An antibody on a particle can only grip the part of a receptor that sticks out of the cell. We measured that fraction for every target named in the project bibliography, using UniProt membrane topology, and asked separately whether the exposed part is conserved between rat and human (which decides whether one reagent serves both the animal model and eventual human work).

The two numbers that decide it P2X3 presents 70.3% of itself outside the cell, in a single continuous 279-residue loop. TRPV1 presents 6.7%, split across four loops of which the largest is 32 residues.

And where every failing target is less conserved on its exposed face than overall (TRPV1 drops 32 points), P2X3 goes the other way, its exposed face is 95.7% identical between human and rat, better than the protein average. Note that TfR itself is only 75.3%, which is exactly why anti-TfR clones cannot be swapped between species.

2 · Antibody shortlist for P2X3

Screened by mining the methods sections of 99 open-access P2X3 antibody papers for cited RRIDs, then resolving each against the Antibody Registry. Five genuine anti-P2X3 reagents were found. Each vendor epitope was checked against the P2X3 sequence, the ectodomain is aa 44–322 of 397.

Read this before ordering anything The most-cited P2X3 antibody in the literature, Alomone APR-016, is raised against aa 383–397, that is the cytoplasmic tail. It cannot bind an intact cell and is useless as a targeting ligand, however many papers use it. It is a perfectly good western-blot and permeabilized-IHC reagent, which is what those papers use it for.

Separately: the epitope Alomone lists is the rat sequence. Human P2X3 differs at two of those fifteen positions (AEKQSTDSGAFSIGH vs VEKQSTDSGAYSIGH).

3 · What to do first

  1. Request immunogen sequences from Thermo for PA5-77682 and PA5-115707. Ask for the peptide, not the datasheet. Check it falls inside aa 44–322. Do not order until you have it.
  2. Live-cell binding test. P2X3-expressing line (or rat DRG primary culture) against a P2X3-negative control line. Stain unpermeabilized, on ice, with a viability dye to gate out dead cells, dead cells take up antibody non-specifically and are the usual source of a false positive here. Read by flow cytometry. This is the gate: if nothing binds the intact cell, the targeting strategy stops here.
  3. Permeabilized control in parallel. Same antibody, same cells, permeabilized. If it stains permeabilized but not intact cells, the epitope is internal, that is an informative negative, not a failed experiment.
  4. Only then conjugate to the particle and repeat the binding test on the formulated lipoplex.

4 · Honest limitations

  • P2X3 has never been used as a nanoparticle targeting ligand. Literature counts: 283 papers on P2X3 as a nociceptor marker, 52 on clinical antagonists (the gefapixant programme), but only 9 touching live-cell surface antibody work and 13 any nanoparticle targeting. The biology is solid; the targeting application is new. Budget for validation, not for citation.
  • Topology is annotation, not structure. The single large P2X3 loop is consistent with the known trimeric P2X architecture, but whether a given epitope is occluded by glycosylation or by the neighbouring subunit is not answered here.
  • 95.7% identity over 279 residues still permits ~12 substitutions. A specific clone's epitope could sit on one of them. This analysis reliably rules targets out; it does not guarantee any particular clone transfers between species.
  • P2X3 is not exclusively neuronal. It is expressed on bladder urothelium and in the airway, the mechanism behind gefapixant's cough indication and its taste side-effects. A P2X3-targeted particle that reaches systemic circulation has off-target tissue.
  • Marker ≠ compartment. As already noted in the project bibliography, a nociceptor marker is not the same thing as a central pain region. Whether P2X3+ peripheral terminals are the right compartment for this delivery route is a separate question this analysis does not touch.
