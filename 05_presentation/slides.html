The therapeutic idea

Make more enkephalin · degrade less of it · at the pain relay

  • Make more: a PENK plasmid expresses proenkephalin → processed to enkephalins → endogenous opioids acting on δ/μ receptors.
  • Degrade less: silence neprilysin (NEP/MME), the enzyme that inactivates enkephalin in seconds, an siRNA knockdown arm.
  • Deliver both together: co-formulated in a lipoplex, delivered locally (intrathecal, spinal dorsal horn).

Opioid-like analgesia without an exogenous opioid. Clinical precedent exists for enkephalin gene therapy in pain.

2 · Restriction-digest QC, before every transfection

Digest A, insert release

AflII + XbaI → 5,345 bp backbone (control) + linker band:

  • ~2.16 kb = IRES
  • ~1.63 kb = P2A

Digest B, identity fingerprint

NcoI (5 cuts) → unique 5-band pattern; second-largest band = gene order.

What it catches

Mis-assembly, wrong linker, wrong gene order, before wasting a transfection.

What it can't

The plasmid-1 point truncation. Gel QC does not replace Sanger sequencing.

Why co-formulation is non-negotiable

Silencing is cell-autonomous, but enkephalin degradation is not.

  • NEP degrades enkephalin extracellularly, on the surface of many cells.
  • siRNA only silences the cells it actually enters.
  • ⇒ Silencing NEP only in the enkephalin-producing cell is insufficient, neighbours still degrade the secreted peptide.
  • ⇒ Co-package siRNA + PENK plasmid in the same lipoplex, delivered to the same locus. This is what makes the lipoplex load-bearing.

Honest limitations & the one-line plan

Stated by design, not hidden:

  • MD lipid topologies are MARTINI-2 analogy models → conclusions are qualitative/comparative, not binding free energies.
  • Cell-autonomy mismatch is the central design risk (mitigated, not erased, by co-formulation).
  • NEP has other substrates (Aβ, substance P) → knockdown kept local & transient.
